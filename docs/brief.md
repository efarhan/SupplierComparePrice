# Project Brief — Supplier Price Compare

## Vision
A lightweight web app that lets Malaysian shoppers and small buyers compare grocery item prices across three suppliers (Klang Import Sdn Bhd, Pasar Malam Trading, JB Wholesale) and instantly see the cheapest source per item.

## Audience
- Home shoppers / market-goers comparing supplier prices (primary, mobile).
- Small buyers who keep a running list of items to price-check.

## Problem
Prices are currently compared by memory or spreadsheets. The app makes the comparison instant and visual: tap an item, type prices, see the winner.

## Success criteria
- A user can add an item, enter prices for all suppliers, and see the cheapest result within 30 seconds, first use, no instructions.
- The cheapest supplier per item is visually unmistakable (green + ★) on every screen.
- Works fully offline, single file, loads instantly on a phone.

## Constraints
- No backend, no accounts, no persistence (sample data only, nothing saved — stated in the UI).
- Single `index.html`, vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no dependencies or build step.
- Currency is Malaysian Ringgit (RM).

## Future direction (out of scope now)
Persistence, more suppliers, item catalog, share/export of results.
